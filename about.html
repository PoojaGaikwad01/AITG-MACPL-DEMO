Overview

Complex parts. Record timelines.

Founded in 1997 by technocrats Ram Bhogale and Ajit Soundalgekar.

MACPL began by making steering columns for scooters and three-wheelers. By 2000 we were known for developing products, and the lines that make them, in record time.

Today more than a dozen OEMs rely on us for steering and suspension assemblies and for critical fabrications for earth-moving equipment.

TPM (Total Productive Maintenance) drives shop-floor efficiency, supported by in-house design and SAP-based administration.

  1. 1997Founded to make steering columns for scooters and three-wheelers.
  2. 2000Known for developing products and their lines in record time.
  3. TodaySteering and suspension assemblies and critical fabrications.

Part of the AITG group

The Applied Innovation & Technology Group began with Nirlep Appliances in 1968. It now spans auto components, steel fabrication, coatings and consumer durables.

Vision & values

Where we aim to go

One vision and six values, shared by every AITG company.

  • 01

    Integrity

    • Integrity means “say what you believe and do what you say”.
    • We hold everyone in the group to the highest standard.
    • We respect the integrity of others as much as our own.

  • 02

    Commitment

    • Commitment is complete devotion to work and responsibility.
    • It shows in every action and makes us a reliable partner.

  • 03

    Compassion

    • Compassion is kind concern for every human being.
    • People are our biggest asset; we value and respect them.
    • We respect everyone, irrespective of capability, gender or class.

  • 04

    Passion

    • Passion is the urge to do more than expected.
    • We strive to go beyond the ordinary.
    • Our efforts aim at sustainable, all-round growth.

  • 05

    Innovation

    • Innovation is generating and exploring new ideas.
    • We pursue the new and unknown without fear.
    • We encourage experimentation at every level, leading to applied innovation.

  • 06

    Proud of roots

    • We are proud of who we are, where we belong and our legacy.
    • We track global trends to anticipate change.
    • We adapt to changing times with our values intact.

Process 02 / 06

Welding and fabrication

Two-stage welding of heavy fabrications

Heavy fabrications are welded in two stages. Child parts are tack-welded to the main component first, which simplifies full welding. Overhead conveyors keep material moving.

  • Tack, then full welding
  • Robotic welding cells
  • Overhead material conveyors
  • In-house Welding Training Centre

In-house Welding Training Centre
